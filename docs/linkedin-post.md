# LinkedIn post draft

Most enterprise AI reports end the same way: a long list of use cases, then the reader is on their own.

So I built Pathwise, an educational prototype that asks what an executive actually needs next: *which of these should we do first, and how do we govern it?*

What it does:
🔎 A filterable library of 60+ original example AI use cases across 9 industries
🧭 A Roadmap Builder that ranks and sequences opportunities into Now, Next and Later, based on industry, priorities, AI maturity and risk appetite
🛡️ Risks and governance controls on every use case, not as an afterthought
💳 A freemium model: a free library, with accounts and a Team plan (Stripe test mode) for saved roadmaps and board-deck export

The most interesting part wasn't the code. It was the product decisions: who pays (strategy teams, not individuals), what stays free (the library as the hook), and how to be honest about the data (everything is labelled as example content).

I built it with AI-assisted development. I owned the strategy, scope and UX, and used an AI coding agent to implement it and test it end to end. It's a great way to go from idea to a working product fast.
